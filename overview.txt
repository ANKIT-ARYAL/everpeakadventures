 <div>The <strong>Classic Everest Trek from Jiri</strong> is a historic journey to Everest Base Camp. This legendary route is the original trail followed by the first Everest pioneers before the airport in Lukla was built. Because it features uncommercialized pathways, deep valleys, and slow altitude gains, this multi-day walking expedition remains an absolute favorite for travelers looking for an authentic, deep immersion into the Solukhumbu region.</div>\r                                               +
 \r                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          +
 <div>What to Expect on Your Historical Journey</div>\r                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      +
 <div>Your mountain adventure begins with a scenic drive from Kathmandu to the trailhead town of Jiri. From there, the footpaths wind gracefully through lush sub-tropical forests, green terraced fields, and over beautiful river suspension bridges. Along the way, staying in quiet local teahouses allows you to experience warm Nepalese hospitality firsthand while building excellent physical stamina for the higher altitude segments ahead.</div>\r                                                               +
 \r                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          +
 <div>Walk Through Authentic Villages to Everest Base Camp</div>\r                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           +
 <div>One of the greatest highlights of the <strong>Classic Everest Trek from Jiri</strong> is walking through traditional ethnic settlements like Bhandar, Sete, and Junbesi that are completely bypassed by modern flights. You will cross the challenging Lamjura La Pass at 3,530 meters before entering the main Khumbu valley at Phakding. This gradual foot journey allows for proper acclimatization, drastically reducing the risks of altitude sickness while exposing you to ancient Buddhist monasteries.</div>\r+
